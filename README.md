A Mario vs Luigi Online Mod.

Includes many new levels (originals, other mods'), along with many custom characters and settings! Not to mention the new amount of power-ups/items to fight with!

## Download

It's still in development, but you are allowed to roam through the source code. However it'd be unethical from you if you grab assets without asking for permission.
Will include Windows, Linux, Mac, and possibly mobile (both Android & iOS) Builds.
